# Team

This section holds lightweight context about the QA department to support development conversations, progression, promotion evidence, team planning, and performance reviews.

Use aliases or initials in filenames and profile headings. Add one file per person from [the team profile template](../templates/team-profile.md) when the roster is available.

## Working Boundaries

- Record work-relevant context, not a diary of every interaction.
- Separate direct evidence, feedback from others, and your own interpretation.
- Date observations and name the source by role or alias where useful.
- Write factual, respectful notes that you could justify in a fair performance conversation.
- Do not record medical information, protected characteristics, or unnecessary personal detail.
- Keep delivery tasks and 1:1 actions in Notion; retain only context that informs development or decisions.
- Revisit stale observations rather than treating them as permanent traits.

## Writing Standard

Anyone can make a subject access request for information held about them, and aliases don't anonymise. Write every people note as if the person will read it.

**Do record**

- Dated, observable facts and their impact on work ("ticket reassigned to LM on 2026-09-18")
- What was agreed, and the support offered
- The person's own words, only when said directly to me and relevant to their work or development
- Evidence for progression and reviews (achievements against objectives; behaviours against the values)

**Don't record**

- Motives, personality labels, or speculation about feelings, confidence, or intentions
- Retention / flight risk, job-seeking, pay or compensation, health, or personal circumstances
- Second-hand comments about a person, or comparisons that rank one person against another
- Ratings, calibration positions, succession views, or details of any formal process (PDP/PIP/capability). These go into the official People templates and systems only; at most, note "formal plan in place — held with People BP"

**Test:** would I be comfortable if this person read it? If not, rewrite it as a fact or leave it out.

### Talk Freely, Record Neutrally

AC can speak plainly to any agent or skill — frustrations, suspicions, blunt language, hunches about someone's motives. That's useful context for advice and should be engaged with honestly, not sanitised or lectured. The filter applies only at the point of **writing to a file**:

1. Use the full, candid input to understand the situation and give advice.
2. When filing, extract only what passes the standard above: the observable facts, dates, impact, agreed expectations, and support.
3. Before writing, list anything that was left out ("Not recorded: speculation about motive; comment about pay") so AC knows what didn't make it in and can take it to the right place (the People BP, the official template, or nowhere).
4. Never quote AC's candid language about a person into a file.

Strategy notes for handling a difficult conversation are fine to think through in chat, but don't file them here.

## Using Profiles

Relevant agents may consult a named profile for review, progression, promotion, goal-setting, or team-movement work. They should:

- distinguish evidence from interpretation
- avoid surfacing unrelated sensitive context
- check dates and flag stale or incomplete information
- never infer a rating, readiness, or personal intent that is not recorded
