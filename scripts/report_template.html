Driving theme

This week
Led by to-dos. A challenge or improvement (CII) is how a problem or idea is tracked; its initiative, if any, is the higher-level work item and rolls up into it. Each open CII in Now or Next is asked: does it have a task? The four tiles summarise this section only: to-dos on you, tasks waiting on others, items needing attention, dated items coming up.Source: Notion To Do snapshot, CII roadmaps
Focus
Your open To Do cards (To Do or Do Now) grouped by the CII they are tagged to. The text is the card title, so every line is a real to-do. Cards tagged to an initiative show under its challenge or improvement. Backlog cards count as having a task but are not shown.Source: reports/notion-snapshot.json, written by the Friday Notion sync
Waiting on
Next roadmap steps (doing or next) owned by someone other than you, grouped by CII.Source: ## Roadmap tables (owner column)
Needs attention
Two groups. Challenges and improvements without a to-do: a Now/Next CII with no open To Do card and either a next step you own or no next step at all. Plan and role items without a challenge or improvement: things you said you would do (30/60/90-day plan) and job-spec responsibilities (JD-) that nothing tracks yet.Source: roadmaps, Notion snapshot, horizons.md, jd: front-matter
Coming up
Anything date-related that isn't a to-do above: calls, deadlines, sessions.Source: state/now.md · Upcoming
Plan
One card per open challenge (CH) or improvement (IMP) in the Now / Next / Later lane set by its horizon. Each card shows its category (focus area: people, ownership, automation, productivity), its initiative (the INI id, title and status, or Needed / Not needed / Not assessed), its status, how many to-dos it has (or who it is waiting on), and its next ticket: the roadmap step marked doing, else next, with a Jira key if one is recorded. Amber dashed cards are plan items (things you said you would do) and role expectations (job-spec responsibilities, category Role, shown in Later) that no challenge or improvement covers yet. Both need a challenge or improvement.Source: challenges/ and improvements/ front-matter and ## Roadmap tables; initiatives/ (origin links); horizons.md (plan items); jd: tags against context/_taxonomy.md (role items)
