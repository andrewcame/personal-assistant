This week
Led by to-dos. A challenge or improvement is how a problem or idea is tracked, through tasks. Each open one in Now or Next is asked: does it have a card? Now work needs a To Do card; Next work needs a Backlog card. The three tiles summarise this section only: to do, items needing attention, dated items coming up.Source: Notion To Do snapshot, roadmaps
Focus
Your open To Do cards grouped by the challenge or improvement they are tagged to. The text is the card title, so every line is a real to-do. Backlog cards count as having a task but are not shown.Source: reports/notion-snapshot.json, written by the Notion sync
Needs attention
Where the roadmaps and Notion are out of step. A Now item with no To Do card, or a Next item with no Backlog card, whoever owns the next step (a step owned by someone else needs a "Monitor …" card). An open card tagged to a record that no roadmap step names (untracked: notion-sync adds it to the roadmap). A card that is Done while its step is not.Source: roadmaps, Notion snapshot
Coming up
Anything date-related that isn't a to-do above: calls, deadlines, sessions.Source: state/now.md · Upcoming
Plan
One card per open challenge (CH) or improvement (IMP) in the Now / Next / Later lane set by its horizon. Each card shows its category (focus area: people, ownership, automation, productivity), its Jira keys if delivery is tracked there, its status from its Notion card (In progress = a To Do card, Open = a Backlog card only, Not started = no card), how many to-dos it has, and its next ticket: the roadmap step marked doing, else next.Source: challenges/ and improvements/ front-matter and ## Roadmap tables; Notion snapshot (card status)
Tasks
Every open roadmap step per challenge or improvement, with its owner, its status, and where it is on Notion: To Do or Backlog (the card's column), Needs card (a doing or next step with no card on the board, including a step whose named card cannot be found), or blank (a later or blocked step: no card expected yet). A step is matched to its card by the exact card title in the roadmap's Card column. Untracked marks an open card tagged to the record that no step names, and Card done a Done card whose step is not; both are fixed by the notion-sync skill.Source: ## Roadmap tables (Card column); Notion snapshot
