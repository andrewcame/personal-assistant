# Ways Of Working

This section captures how work moves through the engineering function and how the QA/Test Engineering team communicates, escalates, and makes decisions.

It is not a meeting-notes archive or task tracker. Use it to understand operating rhythm, information flow, expectations, and where the QA & Test Engineering Manager role fits.

## Three Views

1. **Software delivery lifecycle** - the agreed, org-wide way of working: initiative lifecycle, ceremonies, metrics, and kanban board, as documented by others. Treated as reference source material, updated as newer versions land.
2. **Engineering operating model** - how the wider engineering team functions: recurring calls, planning rhythms, delivery processes, escalation routes, decision forums, and where QA needs to show up.
3. **QA/Test Engineering operating model** - how the test team communicates and operates: team calls, lead alignment, chapter practice, deep dives, health checks, reporting, and standards.

## Agenda Lifecycle

- Keep agendas for recurring calls as part of the operating model.
- Treat agendas for one-off or initial conversations as temporary working documents.
- After an ad hoc conversation, remove the agenda and retain only durable context, decisions, challenges, or follow-ups in the appropriate location.

## Boundaries

- Keep actions in Notion, Jira, or Teams.
- Capture purpose, audience, inputs, outputs, and decision points rather than full notes.
- Link to discovery calls or challenges when a recurring forum reveals a repeated problem.
- Treat this as a living operating map; update it when the rhythm changes.
