It records how each cadence works, what information is presented, what decisions are made, and what output should be retained.

## Cadence Detail

### End-to-End Sync
- **Audience:** QA & Test Engineering Manager and End-to-End Tester (LM).
- **Purpose:** Align on end-to-end testing priorities and progress for the day.
- **My role:** Check in, surface blockers or risks, agree focus.
- **Key inputs:** LM's current test focus, outstanding risks.
- **Key outputs:** Shared daily priorities, risks flagged for follow-up.

### Team Stand-ups (Web 1, Web 2, Web 3, App, Payments)
- **Audience:** Individual squads.
- **Purpose:** Team-level planning and blockers.
- **My role:** Not routinely attended; drop in for background/context ahead of other meetings when needed.
- **Key outputs:** Context for Cross-Team Stand-up or other forums.

### Cross-Team Stand-up
- **Audience:** Leads, Software Engineering Managers, senior leadership.
- **Purpose:** Cross-team updates on progress, actions, and issues.
- **My role:** Provide QA update (capacity, workload, balance); staging health/monitoring and warning signals now reported here.
- **Key inputs:** Team updates, actions, issues, staging monitoring signals.
- **Key outputs:** Shared visibility across teams; staging warning signals surfaced early.

### Weekly Support Ticket Review
- **Audience:** Leads / relevant engineers.
- **Purpose:** Review incoming support tickets and agree a plan to tackle them, or route into initiatives as quick wins.
- **My role:** Represent QA angle on tickets and initiative routing.
- **Key inputs:** Incoming support tickets.
- **Key outputs:** Ticket action plan; candidate quick-win initiatives.

### Weekly Initiative Sync
- **Audience:** Delivery, Product, Software Engineering Managers / Principal Engineers.
- **Purpose:** Review the initiative board at a high level: success of delivered work, progress of in-development items, alignment on next initiatives.
- **My role:** Represent QA readiness/risk on initiatives.
- **Key inputs:** Initiative board status.
- **Key outputs:** Initiative progress alignment; next initiatives agreed.

### Weekly Engineering Senior Leadership Meeting
- **Audience:** Me, DJ (Head of Delivery), GH (Head of Software Engineering), GL (Director).
- **Purpose:** Discuss cross-department themes and concerns at leadership level.
- **My role:** Bring QA themes, risks, and escalations.
- **Key inputs:** Departmental themes and concerns.
- **Key outputs:** Leadership alignment; escalations actioned.

### Initiative Reviews (specific initiatives)
- **Audience:** Relevant leads / engineers for the initiative.
- **Purpose:** First-level discussion on specific initiatives before detailed refinement.
- **My role:** Contribute QA perspective early, per agreement with DJ to involve QA sooner.
- **Key inputs:** Initiative details as they emerge.
- **Key outputs:** Early QA input into scoping; reduced late-stage surprises.

### Pico & Config Weekly Sync
- **Audience:** Config team and stakeholders.
- **Purpose:** Discuss the config team's work and any pain points.
- **My role:** Represent QA/testing angle on config-related pain points.
- **Key inputs:** Config team updates.
- **Key outputs:** Shared understanding of config pain points.

### GL 1:1
- **Audience:** Group Software Engineering Director and QA & Test Engineering Manager.
- **Purpose:** Leadership support, strategic direction, role transition, and quality priorities.
- **My role:** Bring focused progress, risks, strategic changes, and asks for support.
- **Key inputs:** Tactical improvements, strategic foundations, team context, member-critical journeys, and quality evidence.
- **Key outputs:** Decisions, direction, support, and useful themes to capture in leadership-direction.md.

### Direct Report 1:1
- **Audience:** QA & Test Engineering Manager and each direct report (DA, TMW, MP).
- **Purpose:** People health check and space to raise points of note or issues.
- **My role:** Check in on wellbeing, listen for concerns, and agree any follow-up.
- **Key inputs:** Direct report's wellbeing, points of note, issues, and support needed.
- **Key outputs:** Follow-up actions, support commitments, and themes for team profiles/QA Health Summary.

### Weekly Engineering Initiative Progress Alignment
- **Audience:** Software Engineering Managers / leads.
- **Purpose:** Discuss progress on initiatives in the lead-up to 1:1s.
- **My role:** Share QA-relevant initiative progress and risks.
- **Key inputs:** Initiative progress.
- **Key outputs:** Alignment ahead of 1:1s.

### QA Lead Sync
- **Audience:** QA & Test Engineering Manager, DA, TMW, MP.
- **Purpose:** Shared priorities, people context, risks, and standards across the QA Leads.
- **My role:** Share my radar/to-do updates and concerns, invite area updates, then capture and review actions.
- **Key inputs:** My updates and concerns, each lead's area update, and previous actions.
- **Key outputs:** Actions log, themes for team profiles/QA Health Summary, and items to escalate into challenges or decisions.

### Software Engineering Monthly All-Hands
- **Audience:** Wider engineering community.
- **Purpose:** Present or provide agenda items to the community, alongside GH, DJ, and GL.
- **My role:** Contribute QA agenda items / present as needed.
- **Key inputs:** Departmental updates.
- **Key outputs:** Shared understanding across wider engineering community.

### GH (Head of Software Engineering) Weekly Sync
- **Audience:** Me and GH.
- **Purpose:** Ongoing alignment on QA resourcing, environments, staging/app alerting ownership, QA strategy, and AI-assisted testing.
- **My role:** Bring a working agenda via Microsoft Teams Loop ahead of each session; raise QA-relevant risks and proposals.
- **Key inputs:** Resourcing decisions, environment/alerting ownership, AI experiment progress.
- **Key outputs:** Agreed direction on resourcing, ownership, and joint AI initiatives; shared working agenda for the following week.
