# Job Specification: QA & Test Engineering Manager

## Role Summary

| Field           | Detail                                                   |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Department      | IT                                                       |
| Reports To      | Group Software Engineering Director                      |
| Line Management | Yes — three Lead QAs (website, payments, mobile app)     |

## Role Purpose

Lead QA and Test Engineering function. Ensure quality is built into everything shipped and that test automation capability keeps pace with a high-frequency agile delivery team. This is a technical leadership role: grow and manage Lead QAs who matrix into delivery squads, own the automation frameworks and AI-driven testing approaches that multiply the team's capacity, and hold the bar on quality standards and NFR performance across the function.

## The Team

- QA professionals matrix into five delivery squads, organised by technology platform not by squad
- Lead QA: website platform (Web Teams 1 & 2, Commercial squad)
- Lead QA: payments subsystem (specialist)
- Lead QA: mobile app
- Each lead has a small number of practitioners; structure flexes as the platform evolves
- High-engineering-velocity environment — automation is the foundation, not an option
- AI-first team, expected to be at the forefront of agentic testing and AI-assisted QA

## Time Split (approximate)

| Area                                                                | %     |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------|-------|
| People leadership (1:1s, performance, dev planning, hiring)         | ~25% |
| Technical contribution and tooling (frameworks, AI tooling, CI/CD)  | ~30% |
| Quality standards and strategy (NFRs, test strategy, full stack)    | ~20% |
| Squad and delivery partnership (embedding, risk, release readiness) | ~15% |
| Reporting and engineering hygiene (metrics, DORA, defect trends)     | ~10% |

## Key Responsibilities

### People Leadership
- Line manage three Lead QAs: 1:1s, performance conversations, development planning, pastoral support
- Support Lead QAs in managing and developing their own practitioners
- Own team growth and progression through the growth framework; track development goals and support promotion cases
- Contribute to hiring, onboarding, and building a pipeline of QA talent

### Technical Ownership
- Build, maintain, and continuously improve test automation frameworks across the full stack: web, native mobile, APIs, backend services
- Own the supporting infrastructure: harness services for test accounts and seed data, environment parity, automation that runs reliably across UK and international markets without manual intervention
- Lead AI-driven testing adoption: agentic test runs, AI-assisted exploratory testing, automated defect triage, intelligent test coverage analysis
- Ensure quality is embedded in CI/CD pipelines with automated test runs, coverage signals, and quality gates visible to engineers throughout delivery

### Quality Standards and Strategy
- Own the quality standards and test strategy for the function; keep them current, applied consistently across all squads, and evolving with the technology
- Lead the QA Chapter as the cross-functional community of practice for quality, test automation, and knowledge sharing
- Partner with Head of Software Engineering and Principal Software Engineer on NFR performance: uptime, API performance, accessibility, DORA signals

### Delivery Partnership
- Work with Software Engineering Managers and Delivery Managers to ensure squads are well supported; surface quality risks early; ground release readiness in real data
- Shift QA from a production-line step to a shared discipline: quality thinking starts at requirements, acceptance criteria and edge cases defined before code is written, functional test automation co-owned between engineers and QA
- Use agentic AI to generate test boilerplate against requirements so automation coverage grows alongside feature development

### Business Stakeholder Engagement
- Engage Finance, payment partners, and commercial/market teams to agree coverage expectations for business-critical areas
- Provide clear, timely visibility of test coverage and quality posture ahead of significant releases, market launches, and technology transformations — in language that enables confident business decisions, not just engineering sign-off
- Build trusted, proactive relationships with non-engineering stakeholders

## Technology Stack

- Website: Astro-powered
- Mobile: .NET MAUI (iOS and Android)
- Backend: event-driven .NET microservices
- CMS: Payload CMS
- Integrations: Braze, Exerp, payment partners
- Cloud: Azure
- Source control: GitHub
- Planning: Jira, Confluence, Microsoft Teams, Miro

## What Good Looks Like (Person Spec)

### Must Have
- Track record at Lead QA or QA Manager level with line management experience
- Strong test automation practitioner: web, mobile, and API layers; strong opinions on what good looks like
- Hands-on testing experience across web, native mobile (iOS/Android), and APIs (RESTful JSON and GraphQL)
- Genuine hands-on experience with AI-assisted testing or actively exploring agentic approaches
- Technically credible across the full SDLC; can engage with engineers, architects, and delivery managers on quality strategy
- Confident people leader: develops Lead QAs through management and coaching
- Understands quality/CI/CD/engineering velocity relationship; embeds quality signals throughout the pipeline
- Takes NFRs seriously: uptime, API performance, accessibility, security
- Outcome-focused: cares about software that works reliably, not test volume
- Builds trusted relationships with non-engineering stakeholders; translates quality posture into business language

### Desirable
- Experience across web, native mobile (iOS/Android), and backend APIs including RESTful JSON and GraphQL
- Familiarity with .NET, Azure, or event-driven microservice architectures
- Accessibility testing (WCAG 2.1 AA), performance and load testing
- Direct-to-consumer digital business background (app, website, or e-commerce)
- Hands-on experience with agentic AI testing frameworks or tools
- Experience delivering and supporting digital products across multiple markets
