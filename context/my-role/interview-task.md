# Interview Task: 30-60-90 Day Plan Brief

## Format

- 20-minute presentation followed by 15 minutes of Q&A (or questions in-line)
- No prescribed format — use whatever structure best tells the story
- Expected to be directional, not a finished plan

## Attendees

| Alias | Role                                |
|-------|-------------------------------------|
| GL    | Group Software Engineering Director |
| GH    | Head of Software Engineering        |
| DA    | Lead QA Engineer                    |

## What They Were Looking For

How I think strategically, build trust with a team, and balance short-term execution with longer-term transformation. Not just what I would do — what I value and how I would prioritise landing into the role successfully.

## Five Strategic Themes

These are the problems and questions the panel explicitly surfaced. They are the lens through which any goals and early achievements should be read.

### 1. Re-energising the Team
The team had just come through difficult deliveries and a restructuring. How would I re-engage and re-energise the QA engineering team?

### 2. Ways of Working
Ways of working had recently shifted. What is the QA lens on that, and how would I fill in gaps and help the team optimise and adjust?

### 3. Value of Automation
Automation test coverage was ambiguous. Tests were running in the dark with too many intermittent and environmental failures. Does this matter, and how would I coach and drive the team to resolve it?

### 4. Visibility
As a senior leader in the software engineering function, how would I establish myself in the role and build rapport with key stakeholders and leaders across the business?

### 5. What Else
What is on my mind? What questions do I have, and what would I do to find answers and drive excellence? This was an open invitation to show passion and belief beyond the brief.

## Why This File Matters

These five themes are the stated priorities of the hiring panel at the point I was appointed. They represent the most direct signal of what success looks like in the early months of the role. When building goals or reviewing achievements, these themes should be used as a secondary check: does this work address something the business was explicitly asking for?
