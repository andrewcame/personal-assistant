# 90-Day Plan: Build Capability and Momentum

> **Archived onboarding record.** The live plan is now [horizons.md](horizons.md) (Now / Next / Later). Keep this file as review evidence; don't add new progress here.

## Phase Intent

By day 60, automation ownership should be clearly established with the leads and the biggest friction points addressed. Not all problems will be solved, but there should be a clear plan and a visible direction of travel. This phase shifts from stabilisation to building lasting capability.

## Quality Engineering

- Empower QAs to have a bigger say and be trusted — this is the embodiment of the dev and QA collaboration built over the first 60 days
- Rebrand the QA Chapter to the Testing Chapter: open to anyone in engineering, not gatekept to QAs; quality concerns become a shared engineering conversation
- Embed quality by design: not just in refinement but in feature scoping and discovery; encourage partnership with product earlier in the process so that quality thinking happens before a feature reaches refinement
- The aim is for quality ownership to feel natural across engineering, not something imposed by QA

## AI and Tooling

- The first 60 days were about getting the basics right; this phase pushes further with the enthusiasts already identified
- Target the things that slow the team down and elevate testers out of the mundane into work that uses their actual skills
- Run experiments on test case generation, scope analysis, and regression analysis — ground every experiment in outcomes and track what works
- Accelerate the feedback loop on trials to identify what genuinely fits the team; the team's technical acumen is not a blocker here and that is a real advantage
- Ensure any tooling introduced is aligned across engineering rather than becoming one person's effort

## Sustainability

- Favour small, sustainable wins over radical changes that cannot be maintained — new normals beat one-off improvements
- Build and share knowledge: not just domain knowledge but AI knowledge, tooling knowledge, and cross-platform understanding; the internal community of practice should tap into what already exists across the team
- Documentation should serve a purpose and be actively used — explore AI-assisted documentation as one of the experiments, but hold the bar that everything written must be useful
- Formalise development goals for each QA lead against the growth framework; track progress and begin building promotion cases where appropriate; extend this expectation to how leads manage and develop their own practitioners
- Begin building a view of the QA talent pipeline: what does the function need to sustain itself as the business grows across brands and markets? Where are the gaps and how would they be filled?
- The goal is a true matrix organisation: better cover across teams, better cross-team collaboration, and a broader understanding of each other's domains

## Outputs

- A stronger quality engineering function with shared ownership and consistent standards
- Continuous improvement embedded as the operating model, not a project
- NFR coverage visible and actively partnered with engineering leadership
- Development goals formalised for all leads; promotion cases tracked where appropriate
- Hiring and talent pipeline view in place
- A QA function that scales with the business — helping PureGym deliver quickly, confidently, and consistently as it grows across brands and markets
