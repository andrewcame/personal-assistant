## Role

QA & Test Engineering Manager, reporting to GL (Group Software Engineering Director). Line manages three Lead QAs (Website, Payments, Mobile App) who matrix into delivery squads. A technical leadership role: grow the leads, own automation frameworks and AI-driven testing, hold the bar on quality standards and NFRs. → [job-specification.md](my-role/job-specification.md)

Time split: people ~25% · technical/tooling ~30% · standards/strategy ~20% · delivery partnership ~15% · reporting/hygiene ~10%.

## What Success Looked Like At Appointment

The panel asked about: (1) re-energising the team, (2) ways of working, (3) value of automation — tests "running in the dark", (4) visibility with senior stakeholders, (5) what else. Use as a secondary check on goals and evidence. → [interview-task.md](my-role/interview-task.md)

## Driving Theme

**Get off the microsites.** Leadership treats microsite sunset as the precondition for most investment, progression, and growth work; QA's job is to make the Pico-fronted migration safe and fast while keeping people and capability work alive. Front-end-first test strategy is acceptable for this period. The plan itself (Now / Next / Later) is in [horizons.md](my-role/horizons.md); the direction is in [leadership-direction.md](leadership-direction.md). A change to the theme is updated here and in horizons.md together.

## Strategic Frame

Four missions, ten pillars (IDs in [_taxonomy.md](_taxonomy.md); detail in [role-pillars.md](role-pillars.md)):

- **M1 People & Practice** — P1 people capability · P2 knowledge sharing · P3 quality ownership
- **M2 Technical Mastery** — P4 automation trust · P5 environments & data · P9 AI-enabled testing
- **M3 Delivery Flow** — P6 delivery confidence & flow · P7 non-functional quality
- **M4 Business Trust** — P8 critical journey protection · P10 stakeholder visibility

Focus areas: people · ownership · automation · productivity. Objective lenses: People Developer · Value Creator · Business Operator.

## Leadership Decision Lens (short form)

Member value · business enablement (grow, improve, simplify, expand) · proportionate quality at speed · repeatability · ownership close to the work · AI with accountability · people and purpose. Quality makes growth repeatable; it is not friction added after the fact. → [leadership-direction.md](leadership-direction.md#decision-lens)
