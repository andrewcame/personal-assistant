# Competency: Behaviour

## Scale

3 levels: Improve, Core, Star

### Score 1 - Improve
- Description: Understands what the PureGym values mean and shows promise and ability to work towards some or all of the values. Displays a determined work ethic, a punctual attitude regarding meetings, and an ability to work with others effectively.
- Expected example: Accepts feedback about behaviour and makes a visible effort to improve in later interactions. Shows better punctuality, preparedness, or follow-through after concerns have been raised. Works respectfully with others and avoids repeated friction while improvement is still in progress.

### Score 2 - Core
- Description: Understands and demonstrates the expected behaviour of the PureGym values. Can handle conflict appropriately by focusing on the goal at hand while respecting others' opinions. Works well with colleagues regardless of their individual differences, ensuring they include everyone. Has the ability to reflect to become more effective and changes behaviour accordingly.
- Expected example: Handles disagreement in refinement, planning, delivery, or code review by focusing on the outcome and keeping the discussion respectful. Works inclusively across engineering and cross-functional roles, making sure relevant people are involved, heard, and respected. Acts on behavioural feedback and maintains the improvement, showing reliability, professionalism, and good team standards day to day.

### Score 3 - Star
- Description: Consistently upholds and demonstrates the PureGym values throughout every piece of work. Cultivates a great culture, demonstrates openness and transparency, and calls out the elephant in the room, challenging others for change. Advocates for constructive feedback and making sure others are feeling valued and heard.
- Expected example: Creates a healthier squad culture by modelling openness, directness, and psychological safety in day-to-day work. Raises uncomfortable issues constructively when others are avoiding them, helping the team address problems without blame or politics. Actively improves how others feel valued, heard, and included through thoughtful feedback, inclusive behaviour, and consistent reinforcement of strong team standards.
