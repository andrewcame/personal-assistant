# QA Rating Model

## Purpose

Define tester levels, competency set, and how rating scales are applied.

## Tester Levels

- QA Level 1 = Band 1 = Graduate QA Engineer = QA-1
- QA Level 2 = Band 2 = Junior QA Engineer = QA-2
- QA Level 3 = Band 3 = QA Engineer = QA-3
- QA Level 4 = Band 4 = QA Engineer II = QA-4
- QA Level 5 = Band 5 = Senior QA Engineer = QA-5
- QA Level 6 = Band 6 = Senior QA Engineer II = QA-6
- QA Level 7 = Band 7 = Lead QA Engineer = QA-7
- QA Level 8 = Band 8 = Staff QA Engineer = QA-8

## Competencies

1. Technical
2. Collaboration
3. Delivery
4. Leadership
5. People Management
6. AI Accelerated
7. Behaviour

## Behaviour

- Behaviour is tracked separately from the band matrix.
- It is the day-to-day rating used in performance reviews.
- Do not use it to determine band level; use it to assess how consistently someone is living the values in their work.

## Competency Level Names

- Technical: Learn, Execute, Mentor, Manage, Strategise
- Collaboration: Learn, Execute, Mentor, Manage, Strategise
- Delivery: Learn, Execute, Mentor, Manage, Strategise
- Leadership: Learn, Execute, Mentor, Manage
- People Management: Learn, Execute, Mentor
- AI Accelerated: Learn, Execute, Mentor, Manage, Strategise
- Behaviour: Improve, Core, Star

## Notes

- Behaviour is tracked as a separate day-to-day performance review rating and is not shown in the band matrix.
- A dash means the competency is not expected at that band.

## Working Rules

- Use competency definitions from the files in competencies/ when scoring.
- Use levels/ pages for level-specific expectation mapping and calibration.
- Treat this model as a shared team calibration framework, not a personal rulebook for the manager role.
