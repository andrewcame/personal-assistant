Role Purpose

About the Team 

The QA and Test Engineering team is part of PureGym’s Software Engineering function, with a clear purpose: ensuring the quality, reliability, and correctness of the web and app products that acquire and retain members at every PureGym. We’re hyper-focused on five key business outcomes: converting visitors to members, retaining and winning back subscribers, expanding rapidly into new markets, driving acquisition traffic, and supporting PureGym’s low-cost, self-service model. 

The team sits alongside Software Engineering and Software Delivery, with QA professionals matrixed into our delivery squads. The team is organised by technology platform: website, payments, and mobile app. The team operates in a high-engineering-velocity environment, which means automation is required as the foundation of how we maintain quality at pace. We’re AI-first and expect this team to be at the forefront of what agentic testing, AI-assisted exploratory testing, and intelligent test automation make possible. 

The Role 

PureGym’s digital platforms power millions of member interactions and are evolving at pace. As Lead QA Analyst, you are the quality engineering lead for your platform area. You shape the test architecture and automation direction for your platform, provide line management for the QA practitioners within it, and serve as the most senior quality voice within the delivery squads your platform covers. 

You lead through technical authority and people development in equal measure. The test architecture decisions your platform makes, the quality of the reviews you produce, and the growth of the practitioners you develop are all measures of your success. You are the platform’s quality voice in Discovery and at the point the squad commits to an initiative, and you own the quality thread from that commitment through to delivery and close. 

You work in close partnership with the QA and Test Engineering Manager, who drives quality strategy across the whole function. Your role is to bring that strategy to life in your platform and to surface the ground-level picture back to the Manager so their strategic judgment stays connected to reality. 

You’ll be responsible for: 

- Shaping the test architecture and automation direction for your platform: guiding engineers and QA practitioners toward the right decisions on framework structure, coverage strategy, and test tooling, and ensuring those decisions are understood and applied consistently across the squads your platform serves

- Ensuring the test automation infrastructure is reliable and fit for purpose within your platform: working with engineers to maintain harness services that provision test accounts and seed test data consistently, environment parity across dev, staging, and production-equivalent environments, and automation that runs dependably without manual intervention or fragile setup

- Producing high-quality test and automation reviews: thorough, direct, and educational, consistently raising the bar on every piece of work that comes through

- Developing the people in your platform: providing line management for your QA practitioners through regular 1:1s, performance conversations, and development planning through the growth framework; and investing in Senior QA Analysts’ technical growth through pairing, challenge, and honest feedback, giving them thinking tools rather than answers

- Contributing to upstream quality from Discovery through to initiative acceptance: ensuring work arrives with acceptance criteria, edge cases, and test approach already understood; acting as the platform’s quality voice when the squad commits, assessing testability and shaping clarity through challenge and contribution before acceptance rather than after; and owning quality continuity from that point through to delivery, hypercare, and close

- Leading AI-accelerated test engineering within your platform: setting the pace of agentic adoption, directing agents across test generation, exploratory execution, and regression analysis, building repeatable workflows, and coaching the team to work the same way

- Actively managing test debt across your platform’s automation estate: surfacing it clearly, making the case for addressing it, and driving incremental resolution rather than letting it accumulate

- Driving cross-team quality through active chapter participation: sharing automation patterns, raising standards across the function, and improving how quality information flows between platforms

- Holding DORA quality outcomes (change failure rate and mean time to restore) as first-class metrics within your platform; using them as signals of engineering health and leading the response when they deteriorate

- Fostering shared quality ownership within your squads: working alongside engineers to build the habits where testability, acceptance criteria, and test automation are treated as a shared responsibility from the start, not a QA handover at the end

- Holding NFR test coverage within your platform: accessibility, performance, security, and uptime, and escalating clearly when delivery pressure is creating risk to standards the function holds itself to

- Setting the platform’s flow discipline for quality work: ensuring test work does not become a bottleneck; stepping into whatever is needed to keep things moving; and reinforcing the norm that done means deployed and verified

- Working with the QA and Test Engineering Manager on quality strategy and function-level initiatives, and surfacing the ground-level picture from your platform to keep strategic judgment honest

Technology Stack 

Our teams build and run PureGym’s digital estate using a modern, event-driven technology landscape: a fast Astro-powered website, a .NET MAUI native app, event-driven .NET microservices, Payload CMS, and integrations with Braze, Exerp, and our strategic payment partners. It’s a genuinely exciting stack, built for pace, scale, and quality, and the QA function tests across all of it. 

How We Work 

We deliver software through a structured initiative lifecycle (Intake, In Discovery, Ready for Development, In Development, Hypercare and Measurement, and Closed) designed to reduce context-switching, minimise cross-team dependencies, and keep work visible from idea to close. Initiatives are tightly scoped to a short, focused delivery window; anything larger must be sliced before a team takes it on. The delivery window is agreed during Discovery and held firm: scope that won’t fit it doesn’t get accepted when the squad commits. 

Within that lifecycle, squads operate on a two-week sprint cadence using vanilla Scrum or Kanban depending on the nature of their work. Our tooling is practical and widely adopted: Jira for planning and tracking, Confluence for documentation, Microsoft Teams for collaboration, GitHub for source control, and Azure as our cloud platform. Miro when we need to think visually. 

AI is central to how we work, not an add-on. The QA function is in a particularly exciting position here: agentic test generation, AI-assisted exploratory testing, and intelligent defect and coverage analysis are real, fast-moving capabilities. The Lead QA Analyst is expected to be at the forefront of this within their platform, setting the pace of adoption and coaching their team to work the same way.
