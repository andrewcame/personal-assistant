# Review Style Example

This folder contains one full end-of-year review example and smaller section samples.

The aim is to capture your writing voice, structure, and level of detail so future drafts stay close to your natural style.

This is the style reference used by the [Review Writer agent](../../../.github/agents/review-writer.agent.md).

[calibration-top3-examples.md](calibration-top3-examples.md) holds two sets of Top 3 WHAT / HOW entries from another department lead, with notes on what to copy (shape, ticket IDs, outcomes) and what to leave (mechanical phrasing, buzzwords), plus AC's preference for a warmer voice.
