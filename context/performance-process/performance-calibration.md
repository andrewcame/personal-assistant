## The Matrix

Each person gets a 1–3 rating for the **WHAT** (performance against agreed objectives) and the **HOW** (behaviours against the values). They are weighted equally.

> ⚠️ The Manager Guide writes cells as **WHAT:HOW**; the Matrix Summary labels them **HOW–WHAT** (e.g. "Rising Star (3-2)" = HOW 3, WHAT 2). Always write **"WHAT x / HOW y"** in prep notes to avoid swapping them.

| WHAT | HOW | Matrix position                                | Moving forward (summary)                                                        |
|------|-----|------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 3    | 3   | Star                                           | Stretch / promotion consideration; development plan in place                    |
| 3    | 2   | Rising Star                                    | Enrich & stretch; development plan; develop for promotion or lateral move        |
| 2    | 3   | Rising Star                                    | Enrich & stretch; delegate responsibility; develop for promotion or lateral move |
| 2    | 2   | Core                                           | Enrich & stretch; development plan with stretch objectives                      |
| 1    | 3   | Core (performance improvement required)        | Remain in role; performance improvement plan; shadowing                          |
| 3    | 1   | Core (behaviour improvement required)          | Remain in role; development plan focused on behaviours                          |
| 1    | 2   | Improve (performance)                          | Remain in role; performance improvement plan; shadowing                          |
| 2    | 1   | Improve (behaviour)                            | Remain in role; development plan focused on behaviours                          |
| 1    | 1   | Improve                                        | Immediate action; performance improvement plan; consider capability process     |

Meanings: **Star** — exceeds, leads by example · **Rising Star** — over-performs on either WHAT or HOW · **Core** — solid, consistent achiever · **Improve** — inconsistent.

### Position descriptions (Performance Scoring Descriptions Guide)

The People team's description table is labelled **HOW-WHAT**, shown here as WHAT x / HOW y. "Core" on the WHAT means achieving expectations; "high" means exceeding them.

- **Star (WHAT 3 / HOW 3)** — highest level in both what they deliver and how. A constant ambassador of the values and code of conduct, leading by example; exceeds expectations against all goals. *Moving forward:* keep out-performing as the role grows or take on more responsibility; consider promotion against current vacancies or new headcount; extra responsibilities including line management; apprenticeship or further training, including professional qualifications; a mentor; clear development plan.
- **Rising Star (WHAT 2 / HOW 3)** — behaviour at the highest level, performance at the core level. *Enrich / stretch:* ongoing performance and feedback in 121s; delegate more responsibility and stretch objectives; develop for promotion or lateral move (12 months).
- **Rising Star (WHAT 3 / HOW 2)** — performance at the highest level, behaviour at the core level. *Enrich / stretch:* development plan; extra responsibilities including line management; develop for promotion or lateral move (12 months).
- **Core (WHAT 2 / HOW 2)** — good; where we expect people to be, a core member of the team. *Enrich / stretch:* development plan with objectives that stretch within the role; shadow a star performer in a similar role; ongoing 121s.
- **Core, performance improvement required (WHAT 1 / HOW 3)** — behaviour at the highest level, performance consistently below standard. *Remain in role:* performance improvement plan; ongoing 121s; shadow a star performer.
- **Core, behaviour improvement required (WHAT 3 / HOW 1)** — performance at the highest level, behaviour consistently below standard. *Remain in role:* development plan focused on coaching soft skills (behaviour).
- **Improve, performance (WHAT 1 / HOW 2)** — behaviour at the base level expected, performance consistently below standard. *Remain in role:* performance improvement plan; shadow a star performer.
- **Improve, behaviour (WHAT 2 / HOW 1)** — performance at the base level expected, behaviour consistently below standard. *Remain in role:* shadow a star performer; ongoing 121s; development plan focused on coaching soft skills (behaviour).
- **Improve (WHAT 1 / HOW 1)** — likely on, or about to go on, a performance improvement plan; both what and how need to improve. *Immediate action:* performance improvement plan; consider the Performance Capability Management (PCM) process; consider reducing responsibilities (back to basics).

## Fields To Pre-Populate Per Person

1. Top 3 achievements / key impact delivered (the WHAT)
2. Top 3 behaviours demonstrated (the HOW)
3. 2026 end-year WHAT rating
4. 2026 end-year HOW rating
5. Final 2026 end-year calibration rating
6. Future succession
7. Flight risks (retention, performance concerns, succession)
8. Key improvements / development areas
9. Does the individual have a PDP in place?
10. Is the individual part of an existing learning programme?
11. Is the individual under a PIP?

**What this repo helps with:** fields 1, 2 and 8 are drafted from evidence (team profiles, achievements, values), plus a *suggested* WHAT/HOW position with reasoning. **Fields 3–7 and 9–11 are typed directly into the official template** and never saved here (see the [people-records standard](../../team/README.md#writing-standard)).

## Who Attends

The People team facilitates. Calibration is usually attended by Directors and Heads of, so not every line manager is in the room, but input from every line manager is requested beforehand for their direct reports. Managers get notice and are asked to evaluate their team against the matrix first. Notes captured in the session are shared with attendees afterwards.

## Guidance From The Manager Guide

**Before the session**

- Consider the full year, not just recent weeks; use all reviews and check-ins.
- Consider both the What and the How in the rating.
- Bring tangible, specific examples.
- If you haven't managed someone all year, get input from their previous manager.
- Judge against what can be delivered in the person's contracted hours.

**During the session**

- Keep an open mind to peers' feedback.
- Keep it current: examples from this year only, across the full year, not just the latest few weeks or months.
- Facts and direct experience, not opinion or hearsay.
- Output and impact, not effort or good intentions.
- Spend time on borderline cases, not undisputed ones.
- Be fair and honest; call out bias, including your own.

**After the session**

- Own the calibrated rating; don't tell anyone you'd have given a different one.
- Make sure EOY reviews are booked and completed by 15 January 2027 (the guide says 31 December; AC confirmed 15 January for 2026).
- Deliver the message so each person feels valued and driven to achieve.
- Spend as much time on forward development as on looking back, covering both objectives and behaviours.

**Bias check** — the 2026 guides name four biases to consider, with a strong emphasis on inclusion in the session:

- **Affinity bias** — feeling a connection to people similar to us.
- **Perception bias** — stereotypes and assumptions about different groups.
- **Halo effect** — projecting positive qualities onto people without actually knowing them.
- **Confirmation bias** — looking for evidence that confirms our own opinions and existing ideas.

Recency and contrast between team members are not named in the guides but remain good checks in prep.

## Rating Rules From The FAQs

- **Absent more than half the performance year** (any absence-related reason): defaults to Core. If in the business more than half the year with a fair chance to deliver, rate as normal.
- **Started on or after 1 October:** defaults to Core and is not eligible for the bonus scheme. Started earlier and more than halfway through probation: can be rated. Unsure: ask the People team.
- **Still in probation:** no calibration unless close to the end of probation, in which case an EOY review can be done; otherwise follow the probation review process.
- **Not their manager all year:** get feedback from the previous manager; if ratings differ, the current manager decides.
- **Telling the person their rating:** at the EOY review only, not before, so the conversation is fair and takes their self-assessment into account.
- **They disagree:** discuss at or after the review, with specific examples and evidence.

**Don't** let pay or bonus impact, or worry about a difficult message, change the rating.
