# Objective Lenses

Use these lenses to shape and balance objectives, goals, and achievement evidence. They are a conceptual grouping, similar to SMART: the labels do not need to appear in the wording of an objective.

An item can use more than one lens when the connection is meaningful. Do not force all three onto every item.

## People Developer

Improves your own capability or helps another person grow. This includes coaching, progression, knowledge sharing, feedback, and deliberate skill development.

## Value Creator

Creates useful value beyond the normal expectations of the role. This includes improvements, reusable approaches, innovation, and work that benefits a wider group than the immediate task requires.

## Business Operator

Delivers the role's core responsibilities in support of business goals. This is the day job done well: reliable execution, strategic priorities, operational health, stakeholder confidence, and measurable business outcomes.

## How To Use Them

- When setting goals, use the lenses to test whether the overall set is balanced and intentional.
- When logging achievements, assign only the lenses supported by the evidence.
- When preparing reviews, use the lenses as an additional view across the evidence, not as a replacement for goals or company values.
- Keep objective wording outcome-led; include a lens name only when it helps the reader.
