# PureGym Performance Cycle — Reference

_Process reference from HR's 2026 Performance Cycle Central Manager Guide and the Central Managers upskilling deck (3 Nov 2026), both saved in this folder. Process only: no ratings, no individual assessments. Calibration and the matrix are in [performance-calibration.md](performance-calibration.md)._

Resources and market-specific guides: Connect > Library > All Markets – Performance Management Cycle Resources. Questions go to the People Business Partner (Senior People BP in the upskilling deck) or the People Team inbox.

## The Cycle

| When      | Stage                       | What happens                                                                                       |
|-----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Jan-Feb   | Planning and objective setting | Department POAP (Plan On A Page) formed; individual objectives set, confirmed during February     |
| Jun-Jul   | Mid-year check-ins          | Structured conversation on the What and the How for half one; set up half two                       |
| Oct-Nov   | Calibration sessions        | Held ahead of EOY reviews, with Function Leads and the People Team                                  |
| Nov-Dec   | EOY reviews                 | Final evaluation of the What and the How, with a focus on next year's development                   |

Development conversations continue all year against objectives. The approach is the same in every market; the systems differ.

## Why It Is Managed

Keeps everyone working to shared goals and standards aligned to the POAP. It sets clear expectations, identifies strengths and development areas, gives regular constructive feedback, and ties individual work to company success.

## Objectives (the WHAT)

- Objectives come from the department POAP, then line managers set them for each team member. Inputs: job description, POAP, previous reviews.
- They form the "what" in reviews, alongside the day job.
- The objective test is **SMT**: Specific, Measurable, Timebound/Deliverable (the Central Manager Guide's "SMD" is the same test).
  - **Specific:** owned by the person delivering it; tasks inside an objective can make inputs clear.
  - **Measurable:** includes degrees of success, so it is clear what "core" looks like versus "star".
  - **Timebound / deliverable:** achievable deadline, deliverable in a realistic timeframe, trackable.
- Confirmed in February. UK objectives sit in Dayforce.

## Mid-Year Review (June-July)

- Topics: objectives, wellbeing, development; what has been achieved and how.
- Review objectives (done, pending and why), give constructive feedback with examples, set or amend objectives, agree improvement and development areas, set the person up for half two.
- **No calibration and no ratings confirmed at this stage.** No review template required; a follow-up email capturing the main points and actions is recommended.
- Decision flow in the Mid-Year Review Guide:
  1. No clear objectives and still in probation: follow the probation review process (induction pack, People Coordinator); from 3-6 months in, start setting more specific objectives.
  2. No clear objectives and not in probation: set clear objectives, aligned to the job role and team POAP, uploaded to the relevant system; ask the People BP if needed.
  3. Clear objectives but areas to improve before EOY: talk to the People BP about support to put in place before EOY.
  4. Clear objectives and nothing to flag: hold the mid-year performance and development conversation.

## EOY Review (November-December)

- Full review of all objectives and achievements; both manager and team member prepare; a review template is completed to assess the What and the How.
- Cover: what was achieved and how; each objective with feedback and examples; celebrate achievements; improvement and development areas (what could have been done better); next year (aspirations, early objectives, development focus); confirm the performance rating.
- Also consider tenure, role and responsibility level, improvement areas, development areas and future succession.
- **2026 deadline: EOY reviews are completed by 15 January 2027.** The decks give two different windows ("by 31 December" in the calibration guidance, "end of Nov to mid-Jan" in the upskilling deck); AC confirmed 15 January for this year.
- System by market: UK Dayforce, DK TalentSoft, CH and US manual template.

## Giving Feedback

- Positive feedback reinforces desirable behaviour, recognises achievements and celebrates success.
- Constructive feedback addresses behaviours that need changing and outcomes that need improving, using the **AID** method:
  - **Action:** when and where it happened; be factual.
  - **Impact:** how it affected you, the team or the work.
  - **Direction:** guidance or suggestions for future behaviour.
- HR points to a "Giving and receiving feedback" microlearning module.

## PDP And PIP (definitions only)

Individual plans are never recorded in this repo ([writing standard](../../team/README.md#writing-standard)).

- **Personal Development Plan:** employee-driven and proactive; career growth and skills; positive and aspirational; part of reviews or succession planning; goals, skills, training, timelines, mentoring or stretch work; success is progress towards personal and professional goals.
- **Personal Improvement Plan:** manager-driven and reactive; used for underperformance; more formal; starts after concerns are documented; specific concerns, expected improvements, support and deadlines, with regular check-ins; leads to improvement or further formal action; success is meeting defined standards.
- If performance improvement is needed, talk to the Senior People BP first.
